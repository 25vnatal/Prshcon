Design system

Community Heart

Warm, hopeful, human, calm, contemporary, trustworthy. Every screen should feel like a welcoming community, never enterprise software, a bank, or a hospital. When in doubt, choose the option that feels simpler, warmer, and more human.

Buttons

Primary = filled Warm Blue. Secondary = white with blue outline. Ghost = transparent, hover background only. Destructive = soft coral-red, never bright red. All buttons 48px min height, 16px radius.

Illustration style — simple, warm line art. Never abstract blobs.

Simple 2–3 color line illustrations — a small chapel, a sprouting plant, a warm sun. Consistent stroke weight (~3px), rounded line joins, filled with tints of the palette (never flat black).

Used in every empty state and to soften dashboard headers. Built per-screen as needed — this is the style reference, not a full illustration library.

What to avoid

· Dark mode (MVP)

· Glassmorphism / neumorphism

· Heavy gradients

· Dense enterprise-KPI dashboards

· Harsh reds, black backgrounds

· Sharp corners

· Thin gray low-contrast text

· Tiny click targets

· Busy religious motifs

· Gothic or ornate type
